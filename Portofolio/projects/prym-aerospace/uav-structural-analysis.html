01 · OVERVIEW

Structural Evaluation of a VTOL UAV

The project involved contributing to the structural evaluation of a VTOL UAV as part of engineering activities supporting DGCA certification.

Computer-Aided Engineering methods were used to assess the structural integrity of critical UAV components and support design verification before manufacturing.

02 · MY CONTRIBUTION

Engineering Analysis & Support

01
FINITE ELEMENT ANALYSIS

Critical UAV Components

Performed finite element analyses (FEA) on critical UAV components including the arms, centre frame, and landing gear using ANSYS Mechanical.

02
STRUCTURAL ASSESSMENT

Static, Modal & Fatigue Analysis

Conducted Static Structural, Modal, and Fatigue analyses to evaluate structural performance under different loading conditions.

03
ENGINEERING RESULTS

Simulation Result Evaluation

Evaluated engineering results including equivalent stress, total deformation, strain, factor of safety, natural frequencies, buckling behaviour, and fatigue life.

04
MODEL SIMPLIFICATION

Analysis Model Preparation

Simplified complex UAV assemblies by removing non-critical components to improve mesh quality and computational efficiency while preserving analysis accuracy.

05
ENGINEERING SUPPORT

Design Verification & Certification

Supported the engineering team by providing simulation results and technical inputs for design verification and certification activities.
