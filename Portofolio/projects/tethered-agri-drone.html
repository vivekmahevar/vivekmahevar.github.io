PROJECT · 03

Tethered Agricultural UAV – Precision Fertilizer Spraying

Selected and assembled UAV components based on project requirements for an agricultural fertilizer-spraying application. Integrated the frame, propulsion, ground-based fertilizer supply, pump, and spraying system, followed by flight testing of the assembled UAV.

PERIOD DEC 2025 – APR 2026
FIELD UNMANNED AERIAL VEHICLE
APPLICATION PRECISION AGRICULTURE
ASSOCIATION INSTITUTE OF AERONAUTICAL ENGINEERING
01
PROJECT OVERVIEW

An integrated UAV system for agricultural spraying

The project focused on the development and integration of an unmanned aerial vehicle system for an agricultural fertilizer-spraying application.

UAV components were selected and assembled according to the project requirements. The assembled system integrated the aircraft frame, propulsion system, fertilizer supply, pump, and spraying system.

The completed UAV system was subsequently flight-tested to evaluate its operation during agricultural fertilizer spraying.

02
SYSTEM CONCEPT

Tethered fertilizer supply for the airborne UAV

The system uses a tethered arrangement in which the fertilizer supply is maintained on the ground and connected to the airborne UAV.

This arrangement allows the fertilizer supply system to remain ground-based while the UAV carries the spraying system during operation.

03
COMPONENT SELECTION

Selecting components around the system requirements

UAV components were selected based on the requirements of the agricultural fertilizer- spraying application.

The selected components were assembled into an integrated UAV system consisting of the frame, propulsion system, ground-based fertilizer supply, pump, and spraying system.

04
SYSTEM INTEGRATION

Bringing the UAV and spraying system together

The UAV frame and propulsion system were integrated with the fertilizer delivery and spraying components.

The ground-based fertilizer supply was connected to the airborne spraying system through the tethered arrangement, creating a complete operating system for fertilizer spraying.

05
FERTILIZER DELIVERY

Ground-based fertilizer supply and pumping

The fertilizer supply was maintained at the ground level and connected to the UAV through the tethered system.

A pump was integrated into the system to support fertilizer delivery from the ground-based supply to the airborne spraying system.

06
SPRAYING SYSTEM

Integrated fertilizer spraying during flight

The UAV was equipped with a fertilizer spraying system connected to the ground-based supply.

The integrated system was operated during flight testing to demonstrate fertilizer spraying from the airborne platform.

07
FLIGHT TESTING

Testing the assembled UAV in operation

After component selection and system assembly, the completed UAV was flight-tested to evaluate the integrated system during operation.

The testing demonstrated stable flight of the assembled UAV together with fertilizer spraying during operation.
