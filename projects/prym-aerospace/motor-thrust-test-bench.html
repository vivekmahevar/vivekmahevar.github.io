PRYM AEROSPACE · TECHNICAL PROJECT · 03

Structural Analysis of a Motor Thrust Test Bench

Performed the structural evaluation of a motor thrust test bench to verify its structural integrity before manufacturing. The project focused on using Finite Element Analysis (FEA) to assess whether the proposed design could safely withstand the expected loading conditions, enabling engineering decisions on whether the design could proceed directly to fabrication or required further refinement.

01 · PROJECT SCOPE

Structural Validation Before Manufacturing

The objective of the project was to validate the structural performance of the thrust test bench through engineering simulations before manufacturing, thereby reducing design iterations and supporting an efficient product development process.
